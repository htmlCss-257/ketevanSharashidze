Traditional Georgian Food

Georgian cuisine is known for its rich flavors, fresh ingredients, herbs, spices, cheese, meat, and dough-based dishes. Different regions of Georgia have their own traditional foods and cooking styles.

Food is an important part of Georgian culture. Georgian meals are often shared with family and friends around a large table called a supra. A supra traditionally includes many different dishes, wine, conversation, and toasts led by a tamada, or toastmaster. Georgian cuisine also varies between regions. For example, Adjara is famous for Adjarian khachapuri, Samegrelo is known for spicy and flavorful dishes, while Kakheti is particularly associated with wine and traditional meat dishes. Overall, Georgian food is about rich flavors, hospitality, sharing, and tradition. It is one of the most distinctive parts of Georgian culture.
